# Code of conduct

## Our standard

PulseCrawl contributors must keep project spaces professional, inclusive, and
focused on the work. Be respectful of differing experience and viewpoints,
give actionable technical feedback, and assume good faith while still
addressing harmful behavior directly.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of
private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or
affected people.

Report conduct incidents privately through the repository's security-reporting
channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Include only the information needed to
understand the incident. Reports will be acknowledged by a maintainer and
handled confidentially to the extent practical.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories, issues, reviews, discussions, release
channels, project events, and public representation of PulseCrawl.
